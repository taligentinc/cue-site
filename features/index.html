Cue Central Studio

Cue Card & Order of Service Builder

Assemble Sunday AM, Sunday PM, Wednesday, and custom services in builder.php. Drag and drop items, pull in scheduled specials automatically, and publish directly to your team.

  • 16 Item Types: Music (Hymn, Prelude, Choir Special, Choir Opener, Offertory, Special Music), Speaking (Welcome, Prayer, Message, Announcements, Introduction), and Other (Baptism, Birthdays, Dismissal, Custom, Section Break).
  • Templates & Smart Suggestions: Save reusable service templates and auto-pull scheduled specials, offertories, or least-recently-used prelude sets.
  • Conflict Checks & Email Blasts: Automatic blackout date warnings when assigning people, plus one-click Order of Service emails.

Cue Central (Volunteer Portal)

Published Cue Cards & Setlist PDF Viewer

Once a service is published, volunteers view the clean digital Cue Card on their phone or tablet—and musicians can launch a full-screen PDF stack built specifically for their part.

  • Auto-Matched Personal Setlists: Set your preferred hymnal and part tag (e.g., Piano, Soprano) once, and Cue Central builds your PDF stack for the service.
  • Custom PDF Uploads: Need a specific arrangement or marked-up score? Upload private PDFs directly into your personal setlist.
  • Hands-Free Page Turning: Full-screen PDF.js viewer supports left/right screen tap zones and standard ArrowLeft / ArrowRight foot pedals, plus a slide-down Cue Card drawer.
Multi-Hymnal Engine

Hymn Catalog & Usage Metrics

Stop guessing when your congregation last sang a hymn. Cue Central Studio tracks usage history across all of your church's hymnals and surfaces what's overused or ready to bring back.

  • Dashboard Rotation Cards: See Most Used Hymns, Dusting Off (oldest last-sung date), and Never Sung hymns right on your Studio dashboard.
  • Multi-Hymnal Mapping: Configure multiple hymnals per church with custom short codes, priority order, and badge colors.
  • CSV & Bulk ZIP Scan Import: Import your hymn catalog via CSV and upload individual or ZIP-bundled PDF scans.

Choir & Specials Library

Specials Catalog & S3 Sheet Music

Organize every anthem, vocal trio, and instrumental special in one searchable library. Upload PDF sheet music and any rehearsal audio files to S3-backed cloud storage.

  • Status & Tag Tracking: Mark songs as Active, In Rehearsal, or Retired, add custom tags, director notes, and YouTube reference links.
  • Smart Filename Shortcodes: Upload files and let Studio auto-match asset labels (e.g., Piano, Full Score, Soprano, Alto) using your configured shortcodes.
  • Volunteer Sheet Music Browser: Choir members search Specials and Hymns in central_sheetmusic.php to open PDFs or play uploaded audio tracks.
Monthly Scheduling

Master Schedule & Automated Reminders

Plan your entire month on one calendar grid, track volunteer blackout dates, and let Cue Central handle routine follow-ups automatically.

  • Monthly Calendar Grid: Assign Pianists (PIA), Offertories (OFF), Choir Openers (OPN), Choir Specials, Special Music (SPC), and day markers.
  • 6-Day Special Music Request Emails: If a scheduled special doesn't have a song selected 6 days out, Cue Central emails the group a secure link to submit their title.
  • 3-Day Web Push & Days Off: Volunteers receive Web Push reminders 3 days before assignments and can log blackout dates in central_myschedule.php.

Prelude Sets, Rosters, Print & RBAC

Everything else your music office needs: clean printouts for the pulpit and choir room, organized rosters, and fine-grained security.
