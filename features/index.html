Live Teleprompter

Synchronized Stage Cue Cards

Eliminate squinting and missed cues. Cue Central generates high-contrast, large-print stage prompter feeds for choir confidence displays, vocal monitor carts, and pulpit confidence screens.

  • Zero-Distraction Stage Black Mode: High contrast, zero glare, ultra-legible from 40 feet away.
  • Instant Live Updates: If the Pastor or Director calls an audible change, every prompter screen shifts instantaneously.
  • Instrumental / Conductor Notes: Embed modulation reminders, solo entrances, and microphone cues directly onto the screen.
Hymnody Intelligence

Smart Hymnody Metrics & Analytics

Guard your church against hymn repetition and discover forgotten gems. Cue Central automatically records every hymn sung, categorizes them by topic, and surfaces actionable intelligence.

  • Dusty Hymns Radar: Surfaces beautiful hymns your church hasn't sung in 6+ months.
  • Overuse Alerts: Protects your congregation from singing the same four songs every month.
  • Multi-Hymnal Mapping: Majesty, Baptist Hymnal, Great Hymns of the Faith, and custom church hymnals indexed.
