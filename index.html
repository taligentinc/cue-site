Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about Cue Central? Here are the answers to the most common questions from directors and church administrators.

How does Cue Central work for our church volunteers?
Volunteers and choir members access Cue Central through our mobile-optimized Stage Portal (a Progressive Web App). They don't have to download anything from the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. When they log in with their email, they instantly see their upcoming service schedule, PDF music scores, and MP3 practice tracks.
Can we use our existing church hymnal?
Yes! Cue Central supports major published hymnals (including Majesty Hymns, Baptist Hymnal, Great Hymns of the Faith, Living Hymns, and Soul-Stirring Songs), as well as custom hymnals. You can cross-reference page numbers, track song frequencies, and store hymn metadata specifically for your church's books.
Does Cue Central support Bluetooth page-turner foot pedals?
Yes. The Cue Central sheet music viewer supports standard Bluetooth pedal hardware (like PageFlip, AirTurn, and Donner), allowing pianists and organists to turn pages hands-free without leaving their piano bench.
What is the difference between Cue Central and Planning Center?
While generic church management tools are built primarily for modern band setlists and generic volunteer check-in, Cue Central is specifically engineered for church music departments. It features choir library box/shelf inventory, hymnody dust tracking, prelude/offertory timing sets, and dedicated high-contrast teleprompter cue cards for choir lofts and music stands.
Can I cancel or change plans at any time?
Absolutely. There are no contracts or long-term commitments. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription at any time directly from your billing console.
