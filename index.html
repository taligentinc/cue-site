Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers about how Cue Central works for your music director, musicians, and church staff.

How do volunteers and choir members access Cue Central?
Volunteers sign in through your church's workspace URL in any mobile or desktop browser—no App Store download required. They can also install it to their home screen as a Progressive Web App (PWA) to view upcoming assignments, published Cue Cards, monthly schedules, and sheet music.
What is the difference between Cue Central Studio and Cue Central?
Cue Central Studio is the management workspace where directors and coordinators build service orders, manage the monthly schedule, catalog hymnals and choir music, and print cue cards. Cue Central is the streamlined volunteer portal where musicians and choir members see their assignments, days off, cue cards, and PDF setlists.
Can we index multiple church hymnals and import our existing catalog?
Yes. Each church workspace can configure multiple hymnals with custom codes, priority order, and badge colors. You can map a single hymn across multiple books, import hymns via CSV, and upload individual or bulk ZIP-bundled PDF scans.
Does the PDF Setlist Viewer work with Bluetooth page-turner foot pedals?
Yes. The full-screen Setlist Viewer renders your personal service PDF stack and responds to left/right screen taps as well as standard ArrowLeft and ArrowRight keyboard events used by Bluetooth page-turner pedals.
Can I change or cancel my subscription at any time?
Yes. Paid plans are billed monthly via Stripe with no long-term contracts. You can manage or cancel your subscription directly from the Billing tab in Cue Central Studio's Admin Settings.
