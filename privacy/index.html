Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 • Taligent Inc

1. Our Commitment to Church Privacy

Taligent Inc ("Taligent", "we", "our") respects the sensitive nature of church membership records, volunteer contact information, and ministry scheduling data. We will never sell, rent, or monetize your church's roster, choir contact lists, or service history to third-party advertisers.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to operate your organization's workspace, including:

  • Account information (name, church name, email address, password hash).
  • Volunteer and musician directory entries (name, phone number, email, vocal part, instrument).
  • Liturgical and musical assets (hymn numbers, song orders, uploaded PDF sheet music).
  • Transactional payment details processed securely by our PCI-compliant payment gateway (Stripe).

3. Data Security and Isolation

Every church workspace operates within an isolated database context (Row Level Security / PostgreSQL isolation). Data in transit is protected using TLS 1.3 encryption, and data at rest is stored in secure enterprise cloud infrastructure.

4. Data Retention and Portability

Your church retains complete ownership of its data. You may export service history and roster records at any time. Upon workspace termination, church data is permanently expunged in accordance with our deletion policy.

5. Contacting the Privacy Team

For any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your church's data rights, contact us at privacy@taligent.dev or support@cuecentral.org.
